Sound familiar?

Clients are spread across chats.

  1. Client messages are split across WhatsApp, Telegram and email, and a claim question looks the same as a payment chaser.
  2. You answer from memory, in another app, with no record.
  3. Annual reviews slip by.

Recruiting by hand does not scale.

  1. Referrals have run out, so recruiting now means cold outreach.
  2. Researching each person takes too long to do for dozens a week.
  3. Nobody tracks who replied or booked, so you cannot see what is working.

Every viewing is a chat thread.

  1. Every viewing is arranged by back-and-forth with the seller and the buyer.
  2. Reminders are typed by hand. With many chats open, one has gone out with the wrong place.
  3. After an offer, the same details are retyped into each form, and signatures are chased by message.

Recruiting and talks run on cold outreach.

  1. Recruiting new agents means cold outreach, one person at a time.
  2. Filling a talk means chasing people one by one.
  3. Nobody tracks who replied or turned up, so you cannot see what is working.

Brand deals get buried.

  1. Brand deals sit in the requests folders on Instagram and TikTok, and get missed.
  2. Paid-partnership emails are mixed in with everything else.
  3. A brand deal should rank first, but the inbox ranks by what came in last.

Finding creators is manual.

  1. Finding the right creators means searching, vetting profile by profile and copying into a spreadsheet.
  2. Filters match follower counts, not your taste.
  3. Every message is written from scratch, and outreach is tracked by hand.

Showing up on Reddit is slow.

  1. Finding the subreddits where your buyers ask takes hours of scrolling.
  2. Most threads are not worth a reply, and it is hard to tell which.
  3. Replies are written from scratch, and nobody tracks which ones worked.
