Trust.

Last updated 2 November 2026. This page is about the CRM Wisp. For Inbox Wisp and this website, see Privacy · Terms.

The short version

  • Your client data is yours. We run the system and act only on your instructions.
  • It is kept in Singapore, in a separate space for each practice.
  • Every change is logged with who made it.
  • We do not sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train any model.
  • If you leave, we delete it within 30 days.

Who owns the data

You do. You decide what goes in and what stays. In data protection terms, your practice is the controller and Agent Casper is the processor. We use your data only to run your CRM, and only as you instruct.

Where it is kept

The app runs on Railway and the data sits in Supabase Postgres, with the database in Singapore. Each practice has its own separate space in the database, with its own login that cannot see any other practice. Daily backups are kept by Supabase.

How it is protected

  • Connections to the database are encrypted.
  • Each person has their own login, and only people the owner invites can sign in. Wrong sign-in attempts are limited, and passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as text.
  • There are two roles: Owner (team, settings, the AI key) and Approver (works the client book and approves replies).
  • Every change is logged down to the field, with who made it. Owners can read the log and download it.
  • The AI key you connect is stored encrypted.
  • You can download any client's whole file at any time.

The AI

The CRM uses your own Anthropic key. When an AI feature reads client text, that text goes to Anthropic (US) under your account, and your agreement with them applies. This means the database is in Singapore, but the AI reads happen in the US. We do not use your data to train any model, and no screen or support tool at Agent Casper shows your client book.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase (Singapore) stores the data and the backups.
  • Railway runs the app.
  • Anthropic (US) reads text for the AI features, under your own account.
  • Cloudflare provides your address (DNS only). No client data goes through it.
  • Stripe (US) takes payment for the subscription. It receives billing details, never client data.
  • Google and Meta (WhatsApp), only if you connect a Google Sheet or WhatsApp.

We tell you at least 14 days before we add a company to this list that handles your client data.

Keeping and deleting

  • Your data is kept until you ask us to delete it, or you leave.
  • If you leave, we delete your practice's data within 30 days of your last day and tell you when it is done. Backups drop off within 35 days after that.
  • If a payment stops, sign-in is locked but the data is kept, so you can pay again and carry on.

If something goes wrong

If we find a breach that touches your data, we tell the practice owner within 72 hours of finding out.

What we do not have yet

  • A second sign-in step, such as an authenticator app.
  • Security certifications such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001.

Questions, or a security review

Write to delegate@agentcasper.work. We will answer a written security questionnaire, and we will sign a data processing agreement on request.
