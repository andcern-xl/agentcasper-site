Privacy.

Last updated 23 October 2026.

The short version

  • If you use the contact form, we keep your email and message so we can reply to you.
  • We use Google Analytics only if you accept it. If you say no, it never loads.
  • If you use Inbox Wisp and connect Google, it reads mail only from the people you choose, and only to rank what needs you. We drop the email text as soon as it has been read.
  • If you link WhatsApp, it only listens. It never sends a message, never reads groups, and drops the text of a chat as soon as it has been read.
  • When someone new writes to you, it reads their first message once to decide whether they belong, keeps only its decision and a short reason, and asks you when it is not sure.
  • We do not sell your information, use it for ads, or let anyone read your messages.
  • Ask us to see, fix or delete what we hold, any time: delegate@agentcasper.work.

Who we are

Agent Casper runs this website and Inbox Wisp, a personal inbox assistant. If you have a question about your information, write to delegate@agentcasper.work.

What you send us through the contact form

The form asks for your email, and optionally a short note about what takes up your day. We use it to reply to you and to follow up on your enquiry, and for nothing else.

Your enquiry is stored in our database, which is hosted by Supabase in Singapore. Only the Agent Casper team can read it. By sending the form you agree that we may use your email and note this way. You do not have to send anything to read the site.

Inbox Wisp and your Google account

This part applies if you sign in to Inbox Wisp and connect your Google account. Inbox Wisp is the one product that asks for Google data. You do not need it to read this site.

What it asks Google for

  • Your email address and name, to know which account is yours.
  • Read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, delete or change anything in your mailbox.
  • Permission to add events to your Google Calendar (the calendar.events permission). Inbox Wisp never writes to your Google Calendar unless you turn that on. By default it only suggests events and asks you first. You can take an event off again at any time.

What it does with your mail

  • It reads the text only of mail from the people you choose in the app. Newsletters, notifications and other automated mail are skipped even from people you chose.
  • When someone new writes, it reads their first message once to decide whether they belong with the work you told it about. It keeps only its decision and a short reason, never the message. If it is not sure, it asks you and reads nothing more from them until you answer. You can change this in Settings, so it asks you about every new person, or leaves new people alone.
  • When you first connect, it takes a one-time look at recent mail headers so you can pick people: who sent it, the subject line, and who you have written back to (the recipient of your sent mail). It reads no message text for this, and keeps the result for up to 7 days.
  • When you reply to someone you chose, in a conversation that is waiting on you, it reads your reply once, against what that message asked, to keep one sentence about what you told them and to tick the steps your reply covers. The text of your reply is not stored.
  • For each message it reads, it works out a short summary, a short list of steps if the message asks you for things, whether you need to reply, a due date if there is one, and why it matters.
  • It also picks out a few exact details that a summary has no room for, such as an address, an amount, a time or a reference number. It never keeps passwords, one-time codes, or card, bank account or ID numbers.
  • The text of the email is deleted as soon as it has been read. It is not kept, even if reading fails.

What we keep

For 90 days unless you set a shorter time in Settings:

  • The sender, the subject, the short summary, the steps, the reason, the due date and a score for each message.
  • Your notes: one short page, written by Inbox Wisp, that holds the exact details above, grouped by topic and dated, so they are still there after the email text is gone.
  • A short note about each person you chose, written from those summaries.
  • For someone new it judged: their name or address, its decision and a short reason.
  • What you tell it by using the app: thumbs up or down on a priority and the reason, people you add, mute or remove, your answers about new people, calendar suggestions you accept or decline, and how quickly you reply to someone. These are short sentences with no message text.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic (US). To read a message, the text of that message is sent to Anthropic's Claude through its API. The exact details and your notes are also sent to Claude when Inbox Wisp reads your next message or rewrites your notes. Anthropic's commercial terms say it does not train its models on data sent this way. If you add your own Anthropic key in Settings, the text goes to your own Anthropic account instead.
  • Supabase (Singapore) stores the records above. Railway (Singapore) runs the app. Both only process data for us.
  • Google, because you are using your Google account.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with anyone else, use it for advertising, or use it to train any model. No screen or support tool at Agent Casper shows your messages.

How we protect it

Your Google sign-in token and your saved WhatsApp login are stored encrypted. Each person's records can only be read by that person's own login. Connections use HTTPS. We never show your sign-in token or your own AI key back to you, or write them to logs.

Your control

  • Disconnect Google in Inbox Wisp (Connections, then Gmail). This revokes our access with Google and deletes the token we hold.
  • Delete everything it has kept in Settings (What I've read). This removes your summaries, steps, details, notes, what it learned from you, and suggestions.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Or write to delegate@agentcasper.work and we will delete your account and everything linked to it.

Google's rules

Agent Casper's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Inbox Wisp and WhatsApp

This part applies if you link your WhatsApp to Inbox Wisp. You scan a code in WhatsApp (Linked devices), the same way you would link WhatsApp Web, and Inbox Wisp stays connected as a linked device so it can see new messages. This is not an official WhatsApp product, so WhatsApp can disconnect a linked device at any time.

What it does with your chats

  • It only listens. It never sends a message and never replies for you.
  • It reads direct chats only. Groups, status updates and channels are never read.
  • It reads the text of chats with people you chose. When someone new writes, it reads their first message once to decide whether they belong, in the same way as for mail, and keeps only its decision and a short reason.
  • A new message waits in our database for a few minutes (up to 30 minutes if you are away from the app), so a run of short messages is read as one conversation. It is then read and the text is deleted.
  • When you link, it looks once at your chats from the last two weeks that are waiting on a reply. For people you choose later, it uses the recent messages it has seen from them.
  • When you answer someone from your phone, it notes that the chat has been answered. If your message holds a question, an offer or a promise, it is read once to keep one short note, and the text is dropped.
  • The last few messages of each chat, yours and theirs, are held in the app's memory (not saved to disk) so a new message can be read with the conversation before it. They are gone when the app restarts.
  • What we keep is the same as for mail: the sender's name and number, a short summary, steps, a due date and a reason, for 90 days unless you set a shorter time.

Your WhatsApp login

To stay linked, we keep the login that WhatsApp gives a linked device. It is full access to your account, so it is stored encrypted and nothing in the app can show it back to you. Disconnect in Connections (WhatsApp) logs the device out of WhatsApp and deletes it. You can also remove the device at any time from Linked devices on your phone.

Who else sees it

Anthropic (US) receives the text of a chat it reads, as described above. WhatsApp sees a linked device on your account. Supabase and Railway (both Singapore) store the records and run the app.

Inbox Wisp and Telegram

If you connect Telegram, Inbox Wisp sends your morning brief and alerts to your chat with its bot. They hold short summaries (who, what, by when), not the text of your messages. Telegram runs that chat, and Telegram chats are not end-to-end encrypted. You can stop it with the Disconnect button in Connections (Telegram) or by sending /stop to the bot.

Analytics and cookies

We would like to know which pages get read, so we can make the site better. For that we use Google Analytics, but only if you click Accept on the cookie notice. Until then, Google Analytics does not load and sets no cookies.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and records things like the pages you visit, your device and browser, and your approximate location. Google processes this information for us. We use it to see overall patterns, not to identify you.

You can change your mind at any time with . Choosing No thanks stops Google Analytics and removes its cookies from this site.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them whenever you open a page. This happens whether or not you accept analytics.

How long we keep things

We keep an enquiry for as long as it takes to reply and follow up. If you ask us to delete it, we will. Inbox Wisp records are kept for 90 days by default, as described above. A WhatsApp message waiting to be read is kept for up to 30 minutes.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Write to delegate@agentcasper.work from the email address you used, and we will get back to you as soon as we can.
