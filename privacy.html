Privacy.

Last updated 28 October 2026.

The short version

  • If you use the contact form, we keep your email and message so we can reply to you.
  • We use Google Analytics only if you accept it. If you say no, it never loads.
  • If you use Inbox Wisp and connect Google, it reads mail only from the people you choose, and only to rank what needs you. We drop the email text as soon as it has been read.
  • If you link WhatsApp, it only listens. It never sends a message, reads a group only if you choose it, and drops the text of a chat as soon as it has been read.
  • When someone new writes to you, it reads their first message once to decide whether they belong, keeps only its decision and a short reason, and asks you when it is not sure.
  • We do not sell your information, use it for ads, or let anyone read your messages.
  • Ask us to see, fix or delete what we hold, any time: delegate@agentcasper.work.

Who we are

Agent Casper runs this website and Inbox Wisp, a personal inbox assistant. If you have a question about your information, write to delegate@agentcasper.work.

What you send us through the contact form

The form asks for your email, and optionally a short note about what takes up your day. We use it to reply to you and to follow up on your enquiry, and for nothing else.

Your enquiry is stored in our database, which is hosted by Supabase in Singapore. Only the Agent Casper team can read it. By sending the form you agree that we may use your email and note this way. You do not have to send anything to read the site.

Inbox Wisp and your Google account

This part applies if you sign in to Inbox Wisp and connect your Google account. Inbox Wisp is the one product that asks for Google data. You do not need it to read this site.

What it asks Google for

  • Your email address and name, to know which account is yours.
  • Read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, delete or change anything in your mailbox.
  • Permission to read and add events on your Google Calendar (the calendar.events permission). It reads your calendar as described under "What it does with your calendar". It never writes to your Google Calendar unless you turn that on. By default it only suggests events and asks you first. You can take an event off again at any time.

What it does with your mail

  • It reads the text only of mail from the people you choose in the app, plus the two cases below: mail that matches something you are focused on, and anything you ask about in Telegram. Newsletters, notifications and other automated mail are skipped even from people you chose.
  • If you tell it what you are focused on right now (for example travel), it also searches your whole Gmail for mail about that, from any sender, including automated senders such as airlines and hotels. It reads up to 25 matching messages from the last 180 days for each search. It searches when you add or change a focus, and when you press "Search my mail for this now". The words it searches for are written by Claude from your focus. Each message is then read like any other: its text is deleted once it has been read, and only the summary and the exact details are kept. Remove the focus and it stops.
  • If you search for a person in People and press "Search your Gmail", it looks at who sent the mail and the subject line of up to 30 matching messages, so you can add that person. It reads no message text, and nothing about the search is kept.
  • When someone new writes, it reads their first message once to decide whether they belong with the work you told it about. It keeps only its decision and a short reason, never the message. If it is not sure, it asks you and reads nothing more from them until you answer. You can change this in Settings, so it asks you about every new person, or leaves new people alone.
  • When you first connect, it takes a one-time look at recent mail headers so you can pick people: who sent it, the subject line, and who you have written back to (the recipient of your sent mail). It reads no message text for this, and keeps the result for up to 7 days.
  • When you reply to someone you chose, in a conversation that is waiting on you, it reads your reply once, against what that message asked, to keep one sentence about what you told them and to tick the steps your reply covers. The text of your reply is not stored.
  • When a conversation you chose has moved on since an item was made, it reads the newest messages of that conversation again, up to 4 and the first 1,200 characters of each, including the ones written by the other person, to judge whether the item is still waiting on you. It keeps one short sentence about what it found, a note asking "Close this?" when it is not sure, and a code that stops it re-reading a conversation that has not changed. The message text is not stored. If it is sure, it closes the item itself, and a Put back button reopens it.
  • For each message it reads, it works out a short summary, a short list of steps if the message asks you for things, whether you need to reply, a due date if there is one, and why it matters.
  • It also picks out a few exact details that a summary has no room for, such as an address, an amount, a time or a reference number. It never keeps passwords, one-time codes, or card, bank account or ID numbers.
  • The text of the email is deleted as soon as it has been read. It is not kept, even if reading fails.

What it does with your calendar

  • If you allow calendar access, Inbox Wisp reads the events on your main Google Calendar: the title, time, place, the description and who is invited. It cannot read other calendars, and it only reads, unless you turn on adding events.
  • It uses them to show what is on today and tomorrow in your daily summary and on your Inbox page, to warn you when a date in your mail clashes with an event, and to learn how your week usually goes (for example a regular class, who you meet often, where you often are), so it can tell what is worth your attention.
  • It decides what to show. Routine items and anything you mark as not useful are left out. To decide, the details of today's and tomorrow's events and what it has learned about your week are sent to Claude. What it has learned about your week is also sent to Claude each time it reads one of your messages, to judge what matters to you. If you turn calendar reading off, none of it is sent.
  • If you connect Telegram, the calendar lines that appear in your daily summary are sent to Telegram with the rest of the summary.
  • Events are read when needed and are not stored. What is kept: the lines that appear in a summary, and short facts about your week such as "Yoga on Tuesdays around 7am" or "Meets Priya often". A fact such as "Meets Priya often" keeps that person's email address, taken from your invitations. They are kept until you delete them with "Delete everything", which also switches calendar reading off. A learned fact fades on its own if it stops showing up in your calendar.
  • If you press "Suggest times" on a message, or ask the bot to propose times, Inbox Wisp reads when you are busy on your main Google Calendar for the next 12 days (start and end times, and only whether each event has a place, never the title, the place itself or the guests) and works out free times inside the working hours you set. The free times are shown to you as text to copy into your reply. If you ask the bot, those times (and no titles) go to Claude so it can write a short message around them, and the message is sent to your Telegram chat. Nothing is sent to the person who asked. Times you offer can be held for 24 hours so the next person is not offered the same ones: a hold is stored as a start time and an end time only, with the message it was for, and it is removed when it expires, when you release it, or with Delete everything. When you say a client chose a time, a calendar suggestion for that time is made for you to approve (or added at once if your calendar rule is set to automatic), as with any other calendar suggestion. Your appointment hours (days, hours, length, gaps, notice, most per day, travel time) are stored with your settings. "Learn from my calendar" reads the start and end times of your last eight weeks of events, and whether each has a place, to suggest those hours; nothing is saved until you press Save, and nothing from it is kept. None of this runs when calendar reading is off.
  • You can turn calendar reading off under Outputs at any time. It starts on for people who allowed calendar access, and the app tells you once when it begins.

What we keep

For 90 days unless you set a shorter time in Settings:

  • The sender, the subject, the short summary, the steps, the reason, the due date and a score for each message.
  • Your notes: one short page, written by Inbox Wisp, that holds the exact details above, grouped by topic and dated, so they are still there after the email text is gone.
  • A short note about each person you chose, written from those summaries.
  • Reminders you asked for in the Telegram chat: the text, the time, and whether it was sent.
  • For someone new it judged: their name or address, its decision and a short reason.
  • Suggestions it makes about how you work, such as making someone more important. Once a week it counts what you finished, answered, left waiting and rated for the people who write to you most, and the model sees only those counts and names, never a message. A suggestion changes nothing until you say yes, and your answer is kept.
  • What you tell it by using the app: thumbs up or down on a priority and the reason, people you add, mute or remove, your answers about new people, calendar suggestions you accept or decline, and how quickly you reply to someone. These are short sentences with no message text.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic (US). To read a message, the text of that message is sent to Anthropic's Claude through its API. The exact details and your notes are also sent to Claude when Inbox Wisp reads your next message or rewrites your notes. Anthropic's commercial terms say it does not train its models on data sent this way. If you add your own Anthropic key in Settings, the text goes to your own Anthropic account instead.
  • Supabase (Singapore) stores the records above. Railway (Singapore) runs the app. Both only process data for us.
  • Telegram, only if you connect it. It receives the summaries and lists described under Inbox Wisp and Telegram.
  • Google, because you are using your Google account.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with anyone else, use it for advertising, or use it to train any model. No screen or support tool at Agent Casper shows your messages.

How we protect it

Your Google sign-in token and your saved WhatsApp login are stored encrypted. Your notes, what Inbox Wisp has learned about you, and the short note it keeps on each sender are also encrypted in our database, with a separate key for each account, so they cannot be read by browsing it. When our support tools open them, for example to help you with a problem, it is written down first with the reason, and you can see it in Settings under Plan and usage (Who has looked at your data). Message subjects and summaries are not encrypted this way yet. Each person's records can only be read by that person's own login. Connections use HTTPS. We never show your sign-in token or your own AI key back to you, or write them to logs.

Your control

  • Disconnect Google in Inbox Wisp (Connections, then Gmail). This revokes our access with Google and deletes the token we hold.
  • See and correct what it knows about you on the Memory page in Inbox Wisp (or send /memory to the bot). It shows the people on your list, what it learned from your calendar, what you told it, and the sentences of your notes, in a list and as a map. Only you see it. "Not right" on something it learned from your calendar stops it learning that again, "Forget" removes something you told it, "Not right" on a sentence of your notes takes that sentence out, and "Hide" only hides it from the map. "Add" on that page saves a note in your words, the same as typing it to the bot. What you hide or mark "not right" is kept as ids and fingerprints (never the text) so it stays hidden, and Delete everything removes it. "Blur names" hides the names on screen so you can share a screenshot.
  • Delete everything it has kept in Settings (What I've read). This removes your summaries, steps, details, notes, what it learned from you, and suggestions.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Or write to delegate@agentcasper.work and we will delete your account and everything linked to it.

Inbox Wisp and WhatsApp

This part applies if you link your WhatsApp to Inbox Wisp. You scan a code in WhatsApp (Linked devices), the same way you would link WhatsApp Web, and Inbox Wisp stays connected as a linked device so it can see new messages. This is not an official WhatsApp product, so WhatsApp can disconnect a linked device at any time.

What it does with your chats

  • It only listens. It never sends a message and never replies for you.
  • It reads direct chats, and the groups you choose on the People page. A group is never read unless you chose it. Status updates and channels are never read.
  • It reads the text of chats with people you chose. When someone new writes, it reads their first message once to decide whether they belong, in the same way as for mail, and keeps only its decision and a short reason.
  • A new message waits in our database for a few minutes (up to 30 minutes if you are away from the app), so a run of short messages is read as one conversation. It is then read and the text is deleted.
  • When you link, it looks once at your chats from the last two weeks that are waiting on a reply. It also lists your chats from the last 90 days (the name or number, the name the person chose for themselves on WhatsApp or you saved for them, the group name, how many messages and when the last one was) so you can pick who matters. For people you choose later, it uses the recent messages it has seen from them.
  • To help you pick, it reads the most recent messages of a chat once, writes a one-line summary of what the chat is about and whether it looks important, and deletes the text straight away. It keeps the summary and the list of chats until you disconnect WhatsApp or delete what it has kept in Settings. Nothing is chosen for you: a chat only joins your list when you accept it, and you can mute it again at any time.
  • When you answer someone from your phone, it notes that the chat has been answered. If your message holds a question, an offer or a promise, it is read once to keep one short note, and the text is dropped.
  • The last few messages of each chat, yours and theirs, are held in the app's memory (not saved to disk) so a new message can be read with the conversation before it. They are gone when the app restarts.
  • What we keep is the same as for mail: the sender's name and number, a short summary, steps, a due date and a reason, for 90 days unless you set a shorter time.

Your WhatsApp login

To stay linked, we keep the login that WhatsApp gives a linked device. It is full access to your account, so it is stored encrypted and nothing in the app can show it back to you. Disconnect in Connections (WhatsApp) logs the device out of WhatsApp and deletes it. You can also remove the device at any time from Linked devices on your phone.

Who else sees it

Anthropic (US) receives the text of a chat it reads, as described above. WhatsApp sees a linked device on your account. Supabase and Railway (both Singapore) store the records and run the app.

Inbox Wisp and Telegram

If you connect Telegram, Inbox Wisp sends messages to your chat with its bot. Telegram runs that chat, and Telegram chats are not end-to-end encrypted.

  • Your morning summary lists what needs you: a short summary of each item, its subject, the name of the person it is from, and when it is due. It does not contain the text of your messages.
  • Each item has three buttons: Done, On it and Not today. Not today hides the item for 3 days. When you finish or drop an item, Inbox Wisp keeps one short line saying so, so it learns what you deal with.
  • About 5 days after you tap On it, it asks once how it went: Sorted, Still waiting or Drop it.
  • The command /todo lists what is waiting on you. The command /later lists what you set aside, and lets you bring each item back.
  • The command /catchup writes a short recap of where things stand, from the same short summaries, and sends it to your chat.
  • If you type anything else to the bot, it keeps what you wrote as a short note in your own words (up to 600 characters, at most 20 a day) and adds it to your notes. Card numbers, ID numbers and codes are removed first. The note is sent to Claude when your notes are rewritten, but not when your messages are scored. You can read and edit your notes in Inbox Wisp, and Delete everything removes them.
  • If you ask the bot a question (for example what is waiting on you, or what is on your calendar), Claude answers it from your short summaries, your notes and the titles and places on your main Google calendar, and the answer is sent to your chat. If it is not in those, it can search your whole Gmail for the words in your question, only because you asked. It looks at up to 4 matching messages once (sender, subject, date and the start of the text, with card numbers, ID numbers and codes removed), uses them for that one answer and does not keep them. The question is kept as a short record (up to 600 characters, card numbers, ID numbers and codes removed) and the answer is kept with your other messages from the bot. If you ask it to add someone to an event it put on your Google Calendar (for example "include x@y.com in the invite"), it asks who they are, then adds that one email address to that event, keeping the guests already on it, and Google emails them the invite. It only does this for an address you typed yourself in that chat, only for events it added itself, and it changes nothing else on the event. It keeps a note of who the address belongs to (the email and what you called them) in your notes until you delete it. The guest's address and the event are not logged. Apart from that, the reminders and due-today messages it sends to your own chat (described below), and skipping a calendar suggestion you say is already on your calendar, it never sends, changes or deletes anything. It never sends anything to anyone but you, except the invite described above.
  • If you ask the bot to remind you at a time (for example "remind me at 3pm to call the dentist"), it keeps the reminder text (up to 300 characters, with card numbers, ID numbers and codes removed) and the time. The text is stored as plain text, like your to-dos. At that time Inbox Wisp sends the reminder to your own Telegram chat by itself, without you asking at that moment. If a send fails it tries a few times and then gives up. A reminder that is a day late is not sent. Sent and cancelled reminders are deleted with your other records after the retention period (90 days unless you set a shorter time), and Delete everything removes your reminders at once.
  • On the morning an open item is due (from 9am Singapore time), Inbox Wisp sends you that item's card in your Telegram chat by itself, once per item per day and at most five a day. The card has the item's short summary, its subject, the name of the person it is from, and the three buttons (Done, On it, Not today). Done, snoozed and set-aside items are not sent.
  • Only the person who connected the chat can use its buttons.
  • Delete everything removes what Inbox Wisp kept. It cannot remove messages that are already in your Telegram chat. Delete the chat in Telegram to remove them.
  • You can stop it with the Disconnect button in Connections (Telegram) or by sending /stop to the bot.

Analytics and cookies

We would like to know which pages get read, so we can make the site better. For that we use Google Analytics, but only if you click Accept on the cookie notice. Until then, Google Analytics does not load and sets no cookies.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and records things like the pages you visit, your device and browser, and your approximate location. Google processes this information for us. We use it to see overall patterns, not to identify you.

You can change your mind at any time with . Choosing No thanks stops Google Analytics and removes its cookies from this site.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them whenever you open a page. This happens whether or not you accept analytics.

How long we keep things

We keep an enquiry for as long as it takes to reply and follow up. If you ask us to delete it, we will. Inbox Wisp records are kept for 90 days by default, as described above. A WhatsApp message waiting to be read is kept for up to 30 minutes.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Write to delegate@agentcasper.work from the email address you used, and we will get back to you as soon as we can.
