Who these terms are with

Restock Wisp is run by Agent Casper. By subscribing or using it, you agree to these terms. Questions: delegate@agentcasper.work.

What Restock Wisp does

  • It checks the public product pages you add, on a schedule: as often as every 2 minutes, every minute around an announced drop, and less often overnight.
  • It sends a message to your own Telegram chat when a page comes back in stock, sells out or drops to the price you set.
  • It shows prices from a few Singapore shops, and US prices where the same product is listed, in Sources. These are for comparison only.
  • You can track up to 50 pages.

What it does not do

  • It never adds to a cart, buys or pays for anything, or logs in to any shop account.
  • It does not hold stock or reserve items. Whether you get an item is up to the shop.
  • It is not linked to, or approved by, any shop or brand it tracks.

Alerts can be late or missed

Shops change their pages, slow down or block automated checks, and Telegram can be delayed. A page that cannot be read for a while is marked as broken and you are told once. We do not promise that an alert arrives in time, or at all, and we are not responsible for items you did not get or prices you paid.

Prices in Sources

Prices come from the shops' public product lists and from TCGplayer through tcgcsv.com, converted with a daily exchange rate. They can be out of date or matched to the wrong product. Labels like Undervalued or Overvalued are a comparison, not advice.

Using it fairly

  • Add only public pages you are allowed to visit.
  • Do not use it to overload a shop or to get around a shop's limits.
  • We may slow down or pause checks on a shop that asks us to, and we may close an account that misuses the service.
